About the Conference

On behalf of the organizing committee, welcome to the 8th International Conference on Tethers in Space. TiS is a focused platform where researchers working on tether dynamics, control, deployment, and applications can present and discuss their latest findings in depth.

The 2027 edition is jointly held with ISTS 2027, giving participants access to specialized TiS discussions and the broader ISTS program spanning space engineering and science. Presented papers will be eligible for a Special Issue of Acta Astronautica.

Venue — Toyama, Japan

Toyama International Conference Center
1-2 Ote-machi, Toyama City 930-0084, Japan

Accessible by Hokuriku Shinkansen from Tokyo (~2 h 10 min). Approx. 15 min walk from JR Toyama Station. Conference Chair Prof. Hirohisa Kojima was born and raised in Toyama City.

Highlights: Toyama Castle Park, Toyama Glass Art Museum, masu-no-sushi.

Co-organized with ISTS 2027

TiS 2027 is held jointly with the 36th ISTS, organized by JSASS and JAXA. Registration, exhibition, and logistics follow the joint ISTS 2027 process.
