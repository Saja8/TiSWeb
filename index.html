Welcome

On behalf of the organizing committee, welcome to the 8th International Conference on Tethers in Space. TiS is a focused platform where researchers working on tether dynamics, control, deployment, and applications can present and discuss their latest findings in depth.

The 2027 edition is jointly held with ISTS 2027, giving participants access to specialized TiS discussions and the broader ISTS program spanning space engineering and science.

Integrated with ISTS 2027

TiS 2027 is planned as a focused tether-systems meeting inside the broader ISTS 2027 program in Toyama.

Dedicated TiS technical sessions
Shared ISTS 2027 conference week
Toyama International Conference Center
Registration and logistics through ISTS

Focused Community

A dedicated forum for space tether systems, from core dynamics to applied mission design.

Mission Applications

Electrodynamic tethers, momentum exchange, debris remediation, sample return, cislunar missions, and beyond.

Publication Path

Presented papers will be eligible for submission to a Special Issue of Acta Astronautica.

Global Community

Researchers from JAXA, ESA, NASA, and leading universities across Europe, Asia, and the Americas converge at TiS.

Interdisciplinary Scope

Tether research spans astrodynamics, plasma physics, materials science, control engineering, and end-of-life mission design.

Student & Early Career

The conference actively welcomes student presentations and early-career researchers as a next generation of the tether community.

Topics of Interest

Fundamental Research

  • Tether dynamics, modeling, and control
  • New tether materials and structures
  • Space elevators and artificial gravity

Technology Development

  • Deployment mechanisms and flight demonstrations
  • Tethered satellites and tether nets
  • Tethered space robotics
  • Autonomy, simulations, and digital twins

Propulsion and Power

  • Electrodynamic tethers
  • Momentum-exchange tethers
  • Tether propulsion and energy generation

Mission Concepts

  • Cislunar and asteroid missions
  • Sample return missions and tether towing
  • Space debris remediation
  • Flight tests and lessons learned
