Visa Information

Participants needing a visa invitation letter should contact the organizing committee via ISTS 2027 channels. Japan entry requirements vary by nationality — consult your nearest Japanese embassy or consulate well in advance.

Venue & Travel

Toyama International Conference Center — 1-2 Ote-machi, Toyama City, Toyama 930-0084, Japan.

15 min walk from JR Toyama Station
7 min by tram — Kokusai-kaigijo-mae stop
5 min by bus from Toyama Station
5 min by taxi from Toyama Station

Getting to Toyama

Toyama is served by Toyama Kitokito Airport (domestic connections). By rail: Hokuriku Shinkansen via Toyama Station — from Tokyo ~2 h 10 min, from Osaka (Kanazawa transfer) ~1 h 50 min.
