Call for Papers

Original contributions on the science, technology, and mission applications of space tether systems are invited.

Important Dates

MilestoneDate
Abstract submission opensSep 17, 2026
Abstract deadlineDec 17, 2026
Paper deadlineJun 17, 2027
ConferenceJul 17–23, 2027

Topics of Interest

Fundamental Research

  • Tether dynamics, modeling, and control
  • New tether materials and structures
  • Space elevators and artificial gravity

Technology Development

  • Deployment mechanisms and flight demonstrations
  • Tethered satellites and tether nets
  • Tethered space robotics
  • Autonomy, simulations, and digital twins

Propulsion & Power

  • Electrodynamic tethers
  • Momentum-exchange tethers
  • Tether propulsion and energy generation

Mission Concepts

  • Cislunar and asteroid missions
  • Sample return and tether towing
  • Space debris remediation
  • Flight tests and lessons learned

Publication

Papers presented at TiS 2027 will be invited to submit to a Special Issue of Acta Astronautica (Elsevier). Detailed instructions will follow the ISTS 2027 process.

Special Issue — Acta Astronautica

Authors should follow the standard ISTS abstract and paper format. Instructions and templates will be provided through the ISTS 2027 process.
